Hand Wash & Wax

Not every visit needs to be a full detail. Wash and wax is the maintenance service: a careful hand wash to lift surface dirt safely, followed by a real coat of wax for shine and short-term protection.

Book Wash & WaxCall (757) 743-9050

Maintenance, Not A Full Reset

Wash and wax is built for a vehicle that is already in reasonably good condition and just needs to stay that way. It is not a substitute for exterior detailing on a neglected finish, and it will not remove bonded contamination the way a decontamination wash does, or level clear coat the way paint correction does.

What it does well is keep a healthy finish looking sharp between those bigger services, at a price and time commitment that fits a monthly or bi-weekly schedule.

When To Upgrade

If the paint feels rough to the touch after a wash, or has gone six months or more without decontamination, wax will not fix that; book exterior detailing instead. If you want protection measured in years rather than months, ceramic coating is the better investment. We will tell you honestly which service the vehicle actually needs when we arrive.

Step By Step

What To Expect: Wash & Wax

  1. Pre-Cleaning Inspection

    We inspect the vehicle for any visible dirt, grime, or damage that needs special attention, and note any specific areas you want us to focus on.

  2. Exterior Washing

    We wash the exterior using a gentle yet effective car shampoo, with microfiber wash mitts or soft brushes to avoid scratching the paint.

  3. Rinsing

    The vehicle is thoroughly rinsed to remove all soap residue and loosened dirt.

  4. Drying

    We dry the vehicle with soft, absorbent towels, making sure no water spots are left behind.

  5. Tire And Wheel Cleaning

    We use a specialized cleaner and brushes to clean the tires and wheels, removing brake dust, dirt, and grime.

  6. Tire Shining

    A tire shine product is applied for a glossy, like-new appearance.

  7. Waxing

    Once the vehicle is dry, a quality car wax is applied by hand or buffed to a smooth finish, giving the paint a protective layer against contaminants and UV rays that typically holds for three to twelve months.

  8. Final Inspection

    We perform a final inspection to confirm the vehicle meets our standard for cleanliness and shine.

Questions

Wash & Wax FAQ

How is this different from exterior detailing?

Exterior detailing includes chemical and clay decontamination before any protection goes on. Wash and wax skips that step, so it is faster and less expensive, but it will not remove bonded contamination or restore a neglected finish.

How long does the wax last?

A quality carnauba wax typically holds three to six weeks in Florida conditions, up to a few months on a garage-kept vehicle. For longer protection, ask about a sealant or ceramic coating instead.

Can I book this on a recurring schedule?

Yes. Many customers keep a vehicle looking sharp with wash and wax every two to four weeks. Set your preferred cadence in the booking notes and we will confirm a recurring slot.
