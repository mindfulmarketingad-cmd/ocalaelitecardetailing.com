Is Mobile Car Detailing Expensive?

By Ivan · August 19, 2026 · 7 min read · Pricing

People generally expect mobile detailing to carry a convenience surcharge, on the logic that anything which comes to you costs more. In practice it usually does not, and the reason is that a mobile operator carries none of the overhead a fixed shop does.

Why Mobile Is Not A Premium

A commercial detailing bay in Ocala means rent, utilities, insurance on the premises, and enough throughput to cover all of it before anyone is paid. A mobile setup replaces that with a vehicle, a tank, and a generator. The labour and the products are identical; the fixed costs are not.

What you are actually paying for in either case is time. A detail is a skilled person for several hours, and that is the number that will not compress no matter how the business is structured.

What Our Services Cost

  • Wash and wax, from $199, one to two hours
  • Interior detailing, from $199, three to six hours
  • Exterior detailing, from $199, two to four hours
  • Full package, from $299, five to eight hours
  • Engine detailing, from $169.99, one to two hours
  • Headlight restoration, from $179.99, one to two hours
  • Paint correction, from $399, one to two days
  • Ceramic coating, from $999, one to three days

Those are honest starting points for a standard sedan in average condition, not bait numbers. Size and condition move them, which is why we confirm a firm price after seeing the vehicle rather than pretending one figure fits every car.

The cost is hours of skilled labour, not the building it happens in.

Why Quotes Vary So Much

Condition is the biggest driver and the one customers underestimate most. A garage-kept sedan detailed twice a year and a work truck that has never been cleaned are the same vehicle count and wildly different jobs. Pet hair alone can add an hour. Heavy smoke odour can add a stage.

Vehicle size is the obvious one. Three-row SUVs have roughly double the interior surface of a sedan and take proportionally longer.

And scope varies enormously between businesses using the same words. One operator’s "full detail" is a wash and a vacuum; another’s includes decontamination, extraction, and sealant. Comparing the headline number without comparing the scope is how people conclude that detailing is a rip-off.

When Cheap Gets Expensive

The genuine risk at the bottom of the market is damage. Swirl marks from a dirty wash mitt need machine polishing to remove, which costs several times what the cheap wash saved. A soaked engine bay can cause an electrical fault. Over-wet carpet that is never properly dried produces exactly the mildew problem the customer was trying to solve.

A useful question when comparing quotes: ask what happens if something goes wrong. An operator who carries insurance and will say so plainly is quoting a different product from one who will not answer.

Where The Money Actually Goes Furthest

If the budget is limited, protection beats appearance. A well-prepped sealant or a ceramic coating slows the damage that eventually forces a much more expensive correction. Clear coat is finite, and once ultraviolet exposure has broken it down the only remedy is paint.

Maintenance beats rescue too. Regular wash and wax over twelve months costs less than one full reset on a finish that was allowed to go too far, and the car looks better the entire time.
