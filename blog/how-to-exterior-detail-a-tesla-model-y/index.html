How To Exterior Detail a Tesla Model Y

The Model Y washes like a Model 3 with two differences that matter: there is a great deal more glass overhead, and the car is tall enough that reaching the middle of the roof safely becomes part of the job.

Height And Reach

Do Not Reach Across The Roof

Do not lean a mitt across the roof from the side. Dragging it over paint you cannot see is how the worst marring happens. Use a long-handled wash tool or a step, and always work with plenty of lubrication.

Rinse top down and wash top down so dirty water never runs over a panel you have already cleaned.

The Glass Roof Is The Main Event

Why It Spots So Badly

It is a large, near-horizontal glass surface, which is the worst possible shape for mineral-heavy Florida water. Sprinkler overspray and afternoon storms dry on it and leave deposits that ordinary washing will not lift.

What To Do About It

Dry it immediately and completely, before you dry anything else. Then seal it. Glass sealant on that panel does more visible good than almost anything else you can do to a Model Y.

Soft Paint, Same Rules

Pre-soak and foam before contact, two buckets with grit guards, pH-neutral soap, clean media, and no brush tunnels ever. Tesla clear coat marks readily and every shortcut shows up in sunlight later.

Two buckets, clean media, top down. On soft paint the method matters more than the product.

Wheels And Aero Covers

Less Brake Dust Than You Expect

Pull the aero covers if fitted and clean the wheel behind them. Thanks to regenerative braking there is far less brake dust than on a comparable petrol SUV, so this is usually quicker than expected.

Cameras, Charge Port And The Hatch

Clean the B-pillar cameras, fender repeaters and the rear camera gently with a damp microfiber; a dirty rear camera on a hatchback is the one you will actually notice. Keep pressure away from camera housings and the charge port.

Open the hatch and clean the shuts and the rubber seal. On a hatchback this lip collects road grime and then transfers it onto whatever you load, which is a complaint that reads as a cargo problem but starts outside.

Protection Priorities

On a Model Y, in order: seal the glass roof, protect the paint, then worry about the wheels. A ceramic coating covers all of it and makes the weekly wash far less risky on paint that marks easily.
